Neural network

Small neural networks, five trained per step and averaged, learn from about 30 inputs: recent returns, trend, volatility, RSI, MACD, range and calendar{{NET_EXTRA}}. Each one is tested walk-forward. It trains on the first 60% of the history, predicts the next block, retrains with that block included, and repeats. Every number below is from bars the model had not seen.
