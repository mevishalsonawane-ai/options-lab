What went in

1.58 million 1-minute BTC bars, rebuilt into 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h and 24h bars and joined by date and time. Every hour, the network sees only bars that have already closed. On top of the 9 timeframes (109 inputs) came 15 more input groups, 93 inputs in total. The largest were option-surface skew and term structure, the event calendar (Fed decisions, jobs reports, option expiries), stocks, VIX and rates, and on-chain data. The rest were order flow and path shape from the 1-minute bars, DVOL, funding, open interest and long/short ratios, basis and the Coinbase premium, Fear & Greed, other markets, spot ETF flows, the CME basis, stablecoin supply and the perpetual premium index. That makes 202 inputs over 26,215 hours.

More timeframes helped where it matters most: big rises and next-day direction

Scores on the last 3 months, where 0.5 is a coin flip:

  • Big-rise alarm: hourly bars alone scored 0.689 on the development year but only 0.507 in the last 3 months. With all timeframes it held at 0.647, and adding DVOL took it to 0.658.
  • Next-day direction: 0.552 with all timeframes against 0.506 hourly-only.
  • Volatility forecasts: strong everywhere (correlation 0.69–0.72), so more timeframes added nothing there.
  • Short-horizon direction (1 and 4 hours): stayed near a coin flip (0.52–0.53) whatever the network saw.
Which timeframes the network uses
  • Next-day direction: mostly the 24-hour bars, then DVOL, 1h and 6h.
  • Big rises: the 1-minute detail inside the hour and the 6-hour bars, then 1h and DVOL.
  • Big drops: the 12-hour and 3-hour bars.
  • Volatility: the 24-hour bars and DVOL.
  • Next hour: the 30- and 15-minute bars.

No single timeframe carries everything. That is why joining them helps.

More inputs made it worse, so each group had to prove itself

Feeding all 202 inputs cut the big-rise alarm to 0.505 and next-day direction to 0.482 in the last 3 months. Each group was therefore added on its own and kept only if it helped in both halves of the development year. Only DVOL passed.

  • Funding, open interest and long/short, and basis gave small but consistent gains, too small to trust.
  • Options skew, the event calendar, cross-market moves, the CME basis, ETF flows, Fear & Greed and the premium index helped in one half and hurt in the other.
  • On-chain, stablecoins, stocks/VIX/rates and order flow made it worse in both halves.
A better score is not yet money
  • Trading the next-day direction call made +20.8% over 91 trades in the last 3 months, but −81.8% over 345 trades in the development year. The 3-month gain was luck.
  • The weekly straddle switch with the new alarms turned $100 into $80.23 (stacked), against $122.70 with the hourly alarms. The whole gap is one week: from 14 Aug BTC jumped 26%. The hourly alarm flagged that week and bought the straddle (+23%); the new alarm did not and sold it (−23%). Every other week was nearly identical, and before the window both alarm sets made about the same (+120% against +124%).

A 3-month result can hinge on a single event. Judge these on the long record and keep position sizes small enough to survive a missed week.
