Ranked findings (paths under android/app/src/main/java/com/optionslab/app/ unless noted):

1. **IraModel.** CORRECTION (Boss, 14 Oct): the phone uses the smallest model, Qwen2.5 0.5B (IraModel.FASTEST,
   about 491 MB file), not the 3B. Weights are mmapped, about 0.5 GB of reclaimable pages counted in RSS, plus a smaller KV/compute
   cache (tens of MB at n_ctx 2048 with n_batch = n_ctx). It is still the largest single item and worth the same fixes,
   but SharedKnowledgeHost (2), the knowledge index (3) and the recorders (4–7) are relatively more important than first
   ranked. The rest of this item describes the code paths, which are the same for every model size.
   - Loaded by the IraPage preload (IraPage.kt:86), typed answers via rewriteWhenFree (IraHub.kt:2668, 4934, 5053), chat (IraHub.kt:5497, 5505), and Hindi (JarvisVoice.kt:804).
   - Not released on trim. IDLE_MS = 10 min (IraModel.kt:494). n_batch = n_ctx (src/jarvis/cpp/jarvis_llm.cpp:86-97).
   - Fix:
     - unload on BACKGROUND/UI_HIDDEN trim;
     - IDLE_MS 2–3 min, 60 s in market hours;
     - no preload in NSE/MCX hours;
     - rewriteWhenFree must not cold-load in market hours (keep the draft);
     - n_batch 512;
     - llama_free(ctx) when idle.
2. **SharedKnowledgeHost.refresh at init** (SharedKnowledgeHost.kt:68-125). It reads up to 200k findings lines into objects held in lastFindings, which is only used for counts (:216).
   - Fix: stream-count the lines without keeping objects, and run nightly or after the close, not at init. JarvisLearning.kt:262-271 has the same pattern (smaller).
3. **KnowledgeLibrary index** (ira/KnowledgeLibrary.kt:12-19; ira-core Knowledge.kt:51-54, 174-198). The 5.6 MB JSON is decompressed and parsed: about 40–50 MB peak, 15–25 MB retained, rebuilt on every restart (triggered by SharedKnowledgeHost.kt:97).
   - Fix: build lazily on the first research question or nightly; drop it on trim; longer term, a prebuilt offset index.
4. **OrderFlow** (ira-core OrderFlow.kt): 1800-s Bar arrays per tracker (:341), 22–30 trackers, 7–10 MB. sampleOptions allocates a DoubleArray(1800) per read every 250 ms (:794-798; OrderFlowLive.kt:338-342). The OrderFlowLive queue is unbounded (:45, :292-300). The Recorder buffer flushes at 512k chars (:502-534).
   - Fix: in-place z; cap the queue (drop the oldest and count it); primitive column arrays; flush at 128k.
5. **Broker instruments memo** (Broker.kt:995-1045): whole-file readText plus a JSONObject tree, 2–4 MB retained plus the tokenMap, re-parsed after every restart. TickZoneRecorder.kt:89-94 re-filters it every 30 s.
   - Fix: streaming JsonReader, intern strings, cache the per-index nearest-expiry slice once a day, re-layout only when ATM moves.
6. **TickZoneRecorder**: loops at :66-68; QUEUE_CAP 600 s (ira-core TickZone.kt:51); `flows` never pruned (:159) with a day-long TreeMap of sizes; `insts` never cleared.
   - Fix: drain every 60 s with QUEUE_CAP about 90; prune flows/insts after close(); a fixed histogram instead of TreeMap; reuse the StringBuilder.
7. **KiteStream**: paths (:140) and LiveCandles (ira-core LiveCandles.kt:46, 165) grow with every token streamed today; unsubscribe (:441) removes only ticks.
   - Fix: prune paths on unsubscribe; drop a token's candles 2 h after its last tick (never for armed underlyings or open positions).
8. **Diag**: 1500 lines in memory, the whole diary re-encrypted per save (Diag.kt:20, 98-103).
   - Fix: append-only encrypted segments, or 300 lines in memory.
9. **Trim handler** (IraAlgoApp.kt:167-170 → AppHealth.kt:148-158) frees only KB.
   - Proposed on BACKGROUND: unload the LLM, null the knowledge index, drop instrumentsMemo/tokenMap (lazy re-read, off the order path), clear lastFindings, optHist and MoveRecorder summaries, trim Diag, prune paths/candles.
   - NEVER touch the order-flow rings for open positions, the LiveCandles of armed underlyings and open positions, the paper book or the position watch.
   - Rename the counter to "background trims".
10. **Restart priority** (CLAUDE.md rule): after a LOW_MEMORY restart, restore the open positions and their stops and resume the watch FIRST, before any other init. Persist the last N minutes of LiveCandles at each minute close (off the tick path) so the arms don't fall back to REST, which explains part of 12 Oct's 4 s signal-to-sent.

Boss's decision (14 Oct): load the shared knowledge (and the knowledge index) in the BACKGROUND, only AFTER start-up has
finished. The start-up order on every launch and restart is:
1. Restore the open positions and their stops, and resume the watch (CLAUDE.md rule).
2. Bring the stream back, the arms and the paper book.
3. Only then, on a low-priority background thread (Dispatchers.IO with a small parallelism limit, never main, never the
   tick/order threads), load SharedKnowledgeHost and the KnowledgeLibrary index.
Also:
- Answers that need it before it is ready say "still loading" or use the last saved counts; nothing waits on it.
- Async alone does not lower the memory peak, so keep the audit's fixes as well: count findings while streaming without
  keeping the objects (no `lastFindings`), build the knowledge index lazily, and drop both on BACKGROUND trim (rebuilt in
  the background when next needed).
- Under memory pressure in market hours (a trim received in the last few minutes) or battery saver, defer the load until
  after the close.

Decided (14 Oct, Boss asked for a recommendation): BUILD ONCE, LOAD INSTANTLY. No daily APK.
Why not a daily APK carrying the findings: the APK is built and published from the PUBLIC options-lab repo, so personal
findings would become public. A sideloaded app also needs a manual install every morning.

1. **Research knowledge (no personal data):** CI prebuilds the knowledge index (the postings/offsets binary, plus fact
   texts read by offset from the asset) at APK build time and ships it in the APK. The app opens it lazily, with no gunzip of the
   5.6 MB JSON and no rebuild at start. This replaces ira-core Knowledge.kt's runtime build for the bundled pack.
2. **Personal findings and lessons:** each night after the MCX close, while charging (the same slot as GitHubSync's evening
   run), the phone compacts the day's findings logs into ONE small snapshot file: counts, per-arm/kind summaries, and the
   last N items needed by answers. It is encrypted like the other private files.
   - In the morning, SharedKnowledgeHost reads the snapshot in milliseconds. During the day, only today's new lines are
     counted incrementally, in memory, bounded.
   - A copy goes to the private IraAlgo repo with the evening upload (never options-lab).
   - If the snapshot is missing or stale, the background-after-start-up path above is the fallback, using a streaming
     count without keeping objects.
3. Both are dropped on BACKGROUND trim and reopened lazily, and never touch the position watch or the start-up order.
