### Reading it (all timeframes)

- **5 minutes loses** with or without a lock (-$19k to -$120k).
- **15 and 30 minutes**: every lock but the loosest lowers the profit; the loosest (1 / 4) about matches no lock on
  30 minutes (+$172.9k vs +$175.5k without targets) with a smaller fall (-$65.7k vs -$81.5k), but the falls stay twice
  the 1-hour arm's.
- **1 hour (the arm in the app)**: no lock is best (+$165.5k with targets, +$213.1k without).
- **4 hours** is the only chart where a lock helps: 1 / 4 adds +$19k with the targets (+$174.1k, t 2.46) and +$38k
  without (+$252.1k, held-out year +$121.2k), at the same fall (-$42.7k); a tight 1 / 1.5 halves the fall (-$24.8k)
  for about half the profit. Only 59 trades in three years, and it overlaps the Trend 4h arm (+$321.9k), which
  already trades the 4-hour trend.
