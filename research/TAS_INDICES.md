loses time value, so the option P&L would be well under half.

### Reading it

- **The gold arm's version (1 hour, held overnight) does not carry over.** On both indices it loses with sells and
  both ways (BANKNIFTY -5,981 pts, FINNIFTY -5,873 pts); an index day has only six 1-hour candles and the overnight gaps
  jump the stops.
- **Intraday (out by the day's last candle, as IraAlgo trades options)**, the best is **FINNIFTY 30 minutes, both
  ways**: 199 trades in two years, 52% won, +21.9 pts a trade, t 2.23, both years positive (+2,752 / +1,614 pts),
  deepest drawdown -675 pts. FINNIFTY 15 minutes both ways is next (t 1.79).
- **BANKNIFTY is weak intraday**: both ways t about 1.0 on every chart; buys only on 30 minutes is the best (+37.7 pts
  a trade, t 1.51) but the second year made only +622 pts.
- Roughly in option terms: FINNIFTY 30m both ways, ~half of +21.9 pts x 65 = about Rs 700 a trade a lot before
  time decay, ~100 trades a year: around Rs 5-6k a month a lot at best. Not tested on real option prices (expired
  options for these years are mostly not available), and FINNIFTY / BANKNIFTY options are monthly only since Nov 2024,
  so less liquid than NIFTY's weeklies.
- Nothing here is as strong as on gold; a paper trial on FINNIFTY 30m would be the only one worth running.
