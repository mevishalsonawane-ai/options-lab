### Verdict

Every one of the 24 buys-only versions (4 arms x 3 sessions x 2 stop scales) lost over the three years, and only two
were positive in any single year. ORB loses the most (it trades the most: each trade pays about $37 a lot in spread
and commission, and gold's breakouts past a 45-minute range do not follow through often enough to cover it). The
nearest to break-even, ORB Sweep with a half-range stop in London (-$2.3k, t -0.11), is noise. None goes into
IraGoldAlgo; the 1-hour liquidity rule (+$96.8k, t 2.10, research/GOLD_24X5.md) stays its only arm. For reference,
Liquidity 15+5 on gold (research/LIQUIDITY_GOLD.md) buys only: London + New York -$32.6k, India hours +$33.3k (t 1.71).
