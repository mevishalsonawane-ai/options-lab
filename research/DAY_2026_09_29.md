What the list shows:

- The owner's candles are almost all **green candles in the up-move above VWAP**. Of the 29 candles listed, 25 are
  green. The red >50-body candles (09:20, 09:25, 12:20-12:30, 13:10-13:15) are not on the list.
- They come in **clusters after a pullback of 2-3 red candles**: 10:25 after the low, 12:35 after the dip,
  13:40 after 13:25-13:35, 14:25 after 14:10-14:20. That is the same shape as the big-candle pullback.
- What came next was mixed. The best follow-through was 10:25 (+81), 12:35-12:45 (+76..+158) and 13:40 (+96). The
  worst was 09:50 (-60), 12:00 (-35, the day high) and 13:05 (-113). The late candles (13:55, 14:25-14:35) gave
  +8..+25, too little for an option bought at the top of the candle.
- Over the year (BIG_BARS.md) a >50-pt candle's direction is a coin flip (51% green) and the next candle does
  not follow it. 29 Sep was a trend day, so the greens followed through better than usual.

**The big-candle pullback rule (COMBINED.md) on this day**: 15-minute bodies of at least 78 pts (the 80th percentile
of the previous two weeks) and ranges of at least 1.2x ATR: 09:15 (red, no pullback entry), 10:30 and 11:00 (green,
no 40% pullback before the next move), 12:15 (red). The 12:15 red candle's pullback gave a PE idea at 12:59: index
54,277, stop 54,393, target 54,043. Neither was hit, and the 15:10 exit was at about 54,260, +17 index points
(+0.15 R). A bought ATM put would have lost a little to time decay. The 12:45 green candle's pullback (13:39 CE, +2R
by 13:51) came while that trade was still open, so the rule (one trade at a time) skips it.
