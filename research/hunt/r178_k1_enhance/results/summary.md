# R178: can the K1 skip be improved?

**Boss, in short (15 Nov 2026).** No. Worse: K1 itself did **not** hold up on the year before, so the R177 "lead" was most
likely luck.

To avoid fooling ourselves, the six versions were compared on the year BEFORE the one where K1 was found
(**20 Nov 2024 - 19 Oct 2025**, 1 lot, after charges and slippage), with the rule fixed in advance: a version is kept only
if it beats today's rules in both halves of that year.

**No version beat today's rules on any arm** (in both halves), so nothing was carried forward to test.

- **K1 on Liquidity:** on the selection year it cut net from Rs 1.43 L to Rs 88k (565 -> 450 trades; worse in both halves).
  On the last year it had lifted it from Rs 1.18 L to Rs 1.40 L. A filter that helps one year and hurts the year before is
  not a reliable edge.
- The only version that gained anywhere was K2 on R51 (+Rs 14k), but it lost in the second half (May - Oct 2025), so it did not qualify.
- On the last year (shown for completeness, not a test): all three arms, today Rs 6.37 L; K1 Rs 6.50 L; K2 Rs 5.85 L;
  K3 Rs 5.83 L; K4 Rs 5.27 L; K5 Rs 5.46 L; K6 Rs 5.52 L. Only K1 beat today, and that is the year it was found on.

**Bottom line:** the volume-profile trap skip is not an edge on these arms. Today's rules stay as they are. This agrees
with R9, R23, R62 and R150.
