# R52 catalogue: older rejected NSE/BSE index-option rules

Written 15 Oct 2026 for R52 (see `PREREG.md`). This is every distinct older rule for NSE/BSE index options (NIFTY,
BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, SENSEX, BANKEX) that the hunt series (H1-H47, R1-R24, OF1, IV1, X1-X3) and the topic
studies (OBUY_*, LIQUIDITY_*, TAS_*, SCALP*, SWING*, LONG_VOL, STRAD_INDEX and others) rejected, dropped or left on
paper/shadow without adopting it. Rules owned by the parallel sessions are left out: R49 (the ORB family), R50
(zero-to-hero and expiry-hero) and R51 (F1, R44, R29, R28, R27, R41, B2, R45, M4 and the paused app arms).

How it was built: six partial catalogues (A-F) were merged. Near-duplicates became one row, and the rule cell names the
merged keys ("= H4-1, LIQ-8"). Numbers are copied from the write-ups. "pre" is the original design window and "hold"
the original holdout. Rs/day is at 1 lot. Every rule BUYS options (selling rules are marked X).

**Signal source** is given for every row:
- **index**: the CLAUDE.md default.
- **index + chain / IV / VIX context**: option-chain data used as a filter around an underlying signal.
- **option-chart [NON-DEFAULT]**: the signal is read from an option's own price.
- **option-chain signal [NON-DEFAULT: no underlying trigger]**: the signal comes from OI, PCR or IV alone.

The two NON-DEFAULT kinds are reported separately and only Boss could switch them on.

**Priority tiers** (the "close?" column is the original catalogues' word):
- **P1**: the rule came CLOSE, it is a stand-alone rule (not an overlay, sizing or execution tweak on an adopted arm), and
  it is testable on the lake. The lake has index 1-minute bars, index options ATM±10 1-minute with OI and IV, and
  India VIX, from Nov 2024. "IS only" (close only in sample) counts as P3.
- **P2**: Boss proposed it, it is testable, and it is not already P1. "Partial" counts only when Boss defined the entry.
  Boss-proposed overlays are P2.
- **P3**: everything else that is testable. This includes overlays on adopted arms, NON-DEFAULT rules and FAR rules.
- **X**: not testable on the lake (see "Data limits"), or a selling rule.

Merged rows whose parts disagree show both verdicts ("CLOSE (H11) / FAR (H23)"). They take the tier of their CLOSE
part, and that part is the "original" setting in `shortlist.json`.

Counts: **96 P1, 27 P2, 127 P3, 18 X** (268 rows from about 330 catalogue rows). "R52 status" is filled in as rules are
tested.

- the WEEK/MONTH ATM±10 option minutes with OI and IV;
- `index_daily`.

These rules cannot be re-tested on it (all X):

  R1-15, H37-11. The SCALP_1MIN-1 A/D layer and H31-7 R10 are also affected; their other parts stay P3.
- **Stock options** (only partial): H39-1 / H39-2.
- **Index futures:**
  - 1-minute futures OI and volume exist only from Jul 2026 (H38-6 / H38-7).

Caveats for testable rules:
- **Expiry days.** BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY and MIDCPNIFTY have had monthly expiries only since Nov 2024. Expiry-day rules on
  them get about one session a month: H28-2, OBUY_SEARCH-2, LV-6 (BN), R24-4/5 and H42-4.
- **Few signals.**
  - R13-1 (weekly MR touches) and R4-13 (8 dates a year) may give 0-3 events in the window.
  - H31-5 (FOMC/CPI nights), R1-9 and LV-5 are also event-limited.
- **Long indicators** (200DMA, 252-day high, 250-day VIX percentile, the R13 14-week MR) need `index_daily` history
  before Nov 2024, as warm-up only.
- **ATM±10 strikes only.** Wider legs than that cannot be priced.
- **Modelled before, real now.** The SENSEX Liquidity studies (LIQ-11/12) modelled option P&L, and FINNIFTY had only
  39 real-option days. The lake has real options for both.
- **Caches to rebuild.** H1 rows, the H25 and H37 outcome tables, the H32 features and the H42/H35 caches were deleted.
  Each study's own build script rebuilds them.
- **Holdouts overlap the final test.** Most original holdouts (Oct 2025 to Oct 2026, R24 Apr to Oct 2026) overlap the
  R52 final test (1 Apr to 13 Oct 2026), as `PREREG.md` notes. Only paper from 15 Oct 2026 is unseen.
