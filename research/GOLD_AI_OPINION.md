  "Standard" and "Freedom" on 4 hours fall apart in the last year (-$72k / -$55k buys only).
- These are buy-only rules in three years when gold rose ~140%, and the timeframe is picked after seeing all three
  years. Weaker than the Trend 4h arm (+$321.9k, -$39.2k) and about level with the Dip arm (+$131.0k, -$42.3k).
- Two quirks of the script: its preset choice changes nothing (the preset values are written to variables that are
  never read), and its "Buy at $..." text can never appear with the default 2-bar confirmation.
