### Reading it

- Data: bid side only (Dukascopy rate-limited the ask download); the ask is the bid + a typical 0.30 spread.
- London + New York, where gold moves most: loses. Buys only 15+5 -16.2k / +8.4k / -24.8k USD per lot a year
  (Oct-Sep years); 30+5 +1.3k over three years. With short sales it loses more.
- India hours (09:15-15:30 IST), buys only: 15+5 -1.7k / +12.1k / +22.9k per lot (+33.3k in 3 years, 0.7 trades a
  day, t 1.7, 20/36 green months, worst drawdown -9.9k); 30+5 -0.8k / +14.8k / +4.9k. Not positive in all three years,
  and gold roughly doubled over these years, so part of a buys-only result is the trend. Not a proven edge.
- Indian residents may not trade XAUUSD with overseas brokers (FEMA / RBI); the legal route is MCX gold.
