# R143: the three candidates on two years they never saw (Dec 2022 - Nov 2024). Two hold up as loss-cutters; the arms themselves lost money then

31 Oct 2026. Research only. **Nothing in the app or any arm has changed.** Boss: *"Try on last 2yrs of data"* and
*"give all the before after results"*. Pre-registration: `research/hunt/r143_unseen_two_years/PREREG.md`, pushed
before any number.

**Why these two years:**
- Our arm tests already used the last two years (Nov 2024 - Oct 2026). Those months were also used to find the ideas,
  so they cannot prove them.
- The two years before them, **1 Dec 2022 - 19 Nov 2024,** had never been used by R139-R142.
- The three rules were run there **frozen, with the exact cuts found before**.

## The answer

1. **Warning first: the three arms LOST money in those two years**, as they run today (1 lot):
   - R72 Breadth Donchian: -Rs 3.07 lakh;
   - R51 Donchian 20 + Supertrend: -Rs 4.00 lakh;
   - R52 Open outside value: -Rs 1.52 lakh.

   They made their money from Nov 2024 on. That was also the month NSE moved to fewer weekly expiries and bigger
   lots. **Their edge may belong to the market since Nov 2024**, so keep them on paper and watch them closely.
2. **R72 "skip after a very wide day" (FB-R72-A10) held up on the unseen years.**
   - It cut the losing days by 31% (-Rs 6.12 lakh vs -Rs 8.87 lakh).
   - It cut the loss by more than half (-Rs 1.37 lakh vs -Rs 3.07 lakh) and the drawdown from Rs 3.38 lakh to Rs 1.92
     lakh.
   - It did this in **each** year. Over the two years, random skipping of the same number never did as well (2,000
     tries).
   - **In all five periods tested it cut losses.**
3. **R52 "sell at 09:25 if the option is not up 5%" (NP5-52) also held up.**
   - Losing days down 28% (-Rs 3.91 lakh vs -Rs 5.43 lakh), the loss smaller (-Rs 1.16 lakh vs -Rs 1.52 lakh), and the
     worst month smaller.
   - In both years. Over the two years, random exit times never did as well (2,000 tries).
   - **In all five periods it cut losses.** It always costs some profit when the arm is making money.
4. **R51 "skip bad breaks" (BBX-51) did not hold up.**
   - It cut losses a lot, but only because it skips more than half of a losing arm's trades.
   - Random skipping of the same number cut losses even more (p 0.995). The pattern it picks is not real.
5. **Against the pre-registered gates,** all three formally fail "the arm still makes money", because the arm itself
   lost money in those years. No filter could pass that while the arm loses.
   - So FB-R72-A10 and NP5-52 are **proven loss-cutters**, not profit-makers: they make a losing arm lose less, and a
     winning arm lose less in its bad months.

## What this means for Boss

- **The two rules worth keeping:** R72's wide-day skip and R52's 09:25 exit.
  - They lowered losses in every period, including two years they had never seen.
  - Random choices did not do as well.
  - They fit Boss's rule: *losing less comes first*.
- **R51's bad-break skip:** drop it.
- **Bigger point: R72, R51 and R52 lost money in Dec 2022 - Nov 2024.** Before any arm goes live, each should be
  judged on a longer history than the last two years. They are paper-only now, which is right.
- **Nothing is built or changed.** If Boss approves, the next step would be a labelled **paper** switch for each of
  the two rules: one-tap undo, logged in the Autopilot timeline, the arm's plain record kept separate.
