### Reading it (buys only - the owner's rule)

- Every 1-hour version that buys only is positive over three years. Positive in ALL three years:
  - London + New York, 1h held overnight: +7.1k / +20.8k / +65.2k USD per lot (88 trades, 0.1 a day, t 1.8).
  - Almost all day, 1h held overnight: +1.5k / +15.0k / +61.0k (155 trades, 0.2 a day, t 2.2, 22/35 green months).
  - India hours, 1h + 15m: +1.6k / +8.1k / +31.0k (133 trades, t 2.8, worst drawdown -7.2k).
- Trend check (gold went 1,847 -> 4,283): random buys held exactly as long as each rule trade make +0.1 to +2.0
  USD/oz a trade; the rule makes +2.3 to +10.6, so it is ahead of the trend, not only riding it.
- Caveats: few trades (88-155 in 3 years), 12 buys-only versions looked at (the best of 12 is flattered), the
  overnight versions pay swap at a real broker (not charged here), bid + 0.30 spread assumed.
- Indian residents may not trade XAUUSD with overseas brokers; MCX gold is the legal route.
