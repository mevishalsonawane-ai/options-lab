# Source review —14November2026

- [Chung and Bellotti, Evidence and Behaviour of Support and Resistance Levels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07410): abstract reviewed. Their heuristic finds evidence of temporary price barriers, bounce dependence and time decay. It does not validate this detector or establish65% NIFTY option wins after costs.
- [Osler, Support for Resistance](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/epr/00v06n2/0007osle.html): institutional summary reviewed. Predictive behavior varies across FX instruments and signal providers. A reversal forecast is not a guaranteed profitable option trade.
- Repository R89_PDHL_BREAK and R170 include negative confirmation/structure tests; the broader strategy catalog also includes VWAP pullbacks. This study therefore claims only its defined comparison and eight fixed neighbors, not discovery of retests as a concept.
- Earlier confluence65 immediate break/sweep/mean-reversion rules failed. Payoff20's calibrated high-confidence subsets were sparse and did not establish a robust65% edge. Those results are preserved; no neural threshold is changed here in response to later outcomes.

These sources motivate a hypothesis. We do not copy their reported performance
into our backtest, claim to replicate their methods, or infer Indian options
profitability from a different market. This batch tests only declared NIFTY
rules, with the earlier cost/data/execution limitations still applying.
