# App audit against the books (25 Oct 2026)

Boss asked (25 Oct): "find out what all the issues we have in the application", in the light of the books in
`research/books/` (Douglas, Aziz, Heitkoetter, Ozenbas/Pagano/Schwartz/Weber, Bennett, Wyckoff, Wilmott, Chan, Björk).
This report checks the app against those lessons and against plain correctness. The evidence comes from the code at commit
`ec07e2a` (paths under `android/`, line numbers from before this branch's fixes) and from the phone's diagnostics for
19-24 Oct (`diag-2026-10-19..24.json.gz`, private release `diag-2026-10`). Those diagnostics hide rupee figures, so some
amounts below are rebuilt from points × lot size.

**Read this first.** Every arm is on paper (`mode.live = false`, `real_orders_allowed = false` on all six days). No issue
below lost real money. The issues show where real money could be lost once the arms go live, and where the paper record
reads wrongly.

Severity: **High** means it can lose money or protection on a live day, or it corrupts the record. **Medium** means it
gives a wrong picture or weaker protection. **Low** means words or polish.
Status: **Fixed** means fixed on this branch (commit named), with tests. **Proposal** means it is for Boss to decide, and
no code was changed.

## What was fixed on this branch (each change has its own commit and tests)

1. **Slippage is measured on paper (E3).** Kept paper fills are now in the diagnostics. Resting paper stops are measured
   against their trigger and limits against their price. Tests: `SlippageTest`, `FillSlippageTest`.
2. **The arms' book survives a failed read (H8).** It is never set aside for a moment's Keystore hiccup. Test:
   `PassedArmsHostTest.aReadThatFailsForAMomentNeverEmptiesTheBook`.
3. **MCX exits sell only on a stop known to apply to MCX (H9).** Tests: `SegmentLockTest`,
   `McxPaperArmsTest.aLossStopOfAnUnknownAccountNeverSellsAHeldPosition`.
4. **Every switch flip is logged (H7).** "Off" from a paper arm's row is in the timeline and can be undone. The ORB-family and
   Liquidity flips go in the diary. Tests: `AutoChangeTest`, `PassedArmsHostTest`, `LiquidityArmTest`.
5. **The loss stop explains itself (R5).** The message lists positions worst first and flags opposite bets. Tests:
   `LossBreakdownTest`, `SegmentLimitsTest`.
6. **The paper loss limit row is honest and guarded (R4, R3).** The words say it sells, the PIN guards loosening, and Jarvis
   says which paper limits are enforced. Tests: `ControlPagesTest`, `IraHubTest`.
7. **Stream watchdog (H2).** Heartbeats with no prices for 20 s in NSE hours means reconnect. Test: `StreamHealthTest`.
8. **Words (O1, O2).**

None of these adds a trade, raises risk, or changes an arm's entry rule. They lower risk, improve logging and
measurement, or restore an arm's own exit rule on paper (H9).

## For Boss to decide (in order of money at risk once live)

1. **Account stop rule on (R6):** switch the −₹40k / −₹13k rule from shadow to on, and add a paper copy so the paper days
   count. It only lowers risk.
2. **Per-trade risk cap (R1, R8):** refuse an entry whose stop risk is above 2% of capital, or that would take the day
   past its limit. Shadow first on live.
3. **Spread gate and capped limit (E2, E1):** a 0.3% half-spread gate, and h14's +0.5% capped limit for Liquidity. Both
   start in shadow.
4. **Stop look speed when the stream is quiet (H4):** Zerodha's quote first and a time limit on each look. This is a stop-path
   change, so it needs its own round of tests.
5. **Opposite bets between arms (R5):** say no to a second arm's opposite bet on a related index, or give each arm its own
   paper loss budget so one arm's morning does not stop the others.
6. **Weekly loss stop (R7)** and the 6% month (Aziz).
7. **IV logging at entry, then research on an IV filter (V1, V2):** measurement first; any filter needs the research gates.
