### Reading it

- As a direction call, no reading holds up in both years. Year A: the 15-minute "taken" and the "draw" pointed the right
  way (+3.8 to +5.8 index pts over 30-60 min, t about 3) and a sweep pointed the WRONG way (price kept going after
  a sweep, -3.7 / -5.6 pts). Year B: all of it vanishes or reverses ("taken" -1.9 / -2.9 pts, t -2.2). Even at
  best, 2-6 index points is less than a bought option's cost and decay.
- As a filter for the arms: no reading makes ORB, ORB Fresh, ORB Sweep or Range Fade profitable in both years. ORB
  agreeing trades lose about as much as the arm (-108 / -170 a trade vs -123 / -175). The one bright cell, ORB Sweep
  with a recent "taken" level its way (+228 a trade, 50 trades, year A) is -103 in year B.
- Consistent in both years but only as "less bad": Range Fade loses more when it fades against a level just taken
  (-185 / -304 a trade vs -26 / -147 when the taken level is its way), and ORB Sweep / Range Fade lose more when a
  liquidity sweep "confirms" the fade (-198 / -218 and -390 / -233). Sweeps on BANKNIFTY 5-minute bars were not
  reliable reversal signals.
