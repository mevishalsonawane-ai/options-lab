### Reading it

- **On real option prices TAS does not work on NIFTY or BANKNIFTY**, on any chart, with or without targets or a lock.
  The best of the 12 versions per chart (picked after the fact, so flattering) reaches at most NIFTY 30m +Rs 54k a lot
  in two years (t 0.68, deepest fall -Rs 69k, second year -Rs 14k) and BANKNIFTY 30m +Rs 15k (t 0.22, fall -Rs 66k).
  5 and 15 minutes lose on both (NIFTY 5m -Rs 60k, BANKNIFTY 5m -Rs 72k a lot as written).
- **FINNIFTY 30m (estimated rupees)** is the only one clearly positive: +Rs 84.9k a lot as written (t 1.14), +Rs 1.03L
  without targets and with a 1 / 1.5 lock (t 1.52, fall -Rs 46k) - but nearly all of it in the first year (+Rs 86k,
  then +Rs 17k), and the rupees are estimated, not real option prices.
- **SENSEX (estimated)** loses on every chart but 1 hour (+Rs 9.7k as written, +Rs 29k with a 1 / 1.5 lock, t 0.64).
- The profit lock helps a little on the indices (unlike gold): a tight 1 / 1.5 or 1 / 2 lock usually cuts the falls
  and adds a few thousand, but never turns a losing chart into a dependable one.
- Why it fails here and works on gold: an intraday index day gives few candles for a trend tracker, the options lose
  time value while the trend develops, and the index trends far less than gold did in these years.
