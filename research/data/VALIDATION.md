## 3. Expired options (rolling ATM-10..ATM+10, 1-minute) vs NSE F&O bhavcopy

Method: for every sampled bhavcopy date, each contract's whole-day candle is rebuilt from the Dhan minute bars of all offsets carrying that strike, using only contracts that stayed inside the fetched ATM band (±10 index, ±5 stocks) all day, so their minutes are complete. That candle is matched to NSE's bhavcopy row for the same strike and type at the nearest weekly (WEEK) or monthly (MONTH) expiry on or after the date. Only contracts NSE shows as traded are scored. *High/low/open match*: within 0.05 or 0.5%. *Last price*: Dhan's last minute close vs NSE `LastPric`. That column only exists in the UDiFF bhavcopy, from 8 Jul 2024; the old format's `CLOSE` is not a last-trade price. *OI*: Dhan's OI on the last minute bar vs NSE end-of-day OI.

## 6. Bad ticks: one-minute price spikes in option files

A *spike* is a contract-minute whose close is more than 50% above both neighbouring closes, or below both by more than a factor 1.5, while the two neighbours are within 50% of each other (the price reverts). Neighbours are the previous and next bar of the same strike and side on the same day (no-trade minutes are skipped); a sustained jump, or a spike lasting two minutes, is not counted. Rows are de-duplicated on (ts, strike, side); zero/missing prices are left out. Share = spikes / contract-minutes. *Early* = 09:15-10:44 IST for NSE/BSE, 09:00-10:44 (the first 105 minutes) for MCX; *late* = 10:45 onward. Exact definition and tests: `spikes.py`, `test_spikes.py`.
