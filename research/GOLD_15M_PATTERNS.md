### Verdict (15 minutes)

- Still close to a coin toss: no hour, weekday, candle size, wick or 1-hour trend moves the next candle far from half.
- The red-run bounce is a little stronger than on 5 minutes (RRRR next green 53.8% / 56.2%, RRRRR 53.7% / 60.2%),
  and green runs still do not continue (GGG 50.0% / 46.7%).
- As a trade it does not hold: buying after 3-5 reds LOST in the fitting years at every holding time but two
  (best +$17.9k and +$11.4k, t 0.5); it made money only in the held-out year (up to +$81.8k, t 1.9), the year gold
  rose fastest - a bull-market bounce, not a pattern that repeats.
- The rule search: 18 of 2,348 buy rules passed the fitting years (fewer than the ~50 chance alone gives at t >= 2),
  and only 2 of those were positive in the held-out year (+$0.4k t 0.01, +$28.5k t 0.47). Nothing to trade.
