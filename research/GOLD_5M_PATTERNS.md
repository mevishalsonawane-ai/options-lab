### Verdict

- Colour is a coin toss: 50.5% of 5-minute candles are green; no hour (49.0-52.7%) or weekday (50.2-50.9%) moves it
  far from half, and neither does the last candle's colour, size, wicks or the 1-hour trend (49.1-52.8% next green).
- The one pattern that repeats in both periods: after a run of red candles the next is a little more often green
  (RR 51.7% / 52.4%, RRR 52.2% / 53.5%, RRRR 52.3% / 55.1%) - a small bounce after a short sell-off. A run of greens
  does NOT continue (GGGG next green 49.6% / 47.4%).
- That bounce is worth about $0.06-0.15 an ounce over the next 1-3 candles; a trade costs about $0.37 (spread and
  commission). Of 2,970 buy rules (every 1-5 candle run x 1-hour trend x session x holding 1-12 candles), none
  even passed the fitting years (positive with t >= 2) after costs. As research/GOLD_SCALP.md found: on 5-minute
  gold the moves are real but too small and too random to pay the spread.
- So no 5-minute colour rule for IraGoldAlgo. Where the red-run bounce could still help: timing an entry the slower
  arms have already decided on (buying after 3-4 red candles rather than at once) - a few cents an ounce at best.
