On Sunday 25 Oct 2026 ten app tests failed in CI that were green on Thursday: the JVM tests ran on the machine's clock, and at a weekend the wall clock's date (Sunday) was two days after the market clock the test rule pins (noon on Friday, the last trading day), so dates built from one clock were compared with dates from the other.
Reproduced locally on the untouched commit with the real Sunday clock: exactly the same 10 failures (ArmLiveTest 2, LiquidityArmLiveTest 1, LiveOrderPathsE2ETest 1, ExactChargesLiveTest 2, TickZoneRecorderTest 4).
Fixed in the TESTS: MarketClockRule pins its noon-on-a-trading-day clock from the moment the class loads (and TestApp pins it before each test instance is built) and restores it after each test instead of the wall clock, so a test class's fields read the same day as its body; AutomationSupport.earlierToday and the fake Zerodha's order, fill and GTT times come from the market clock; every LocalDate.now / ZonedDateTime.now that meant "today" in the app tests now reads Market.today or Market.now.
Fixed in the APP (no change on the phone, where Market.now is the system clock): Broker.findRecent, the exit coordinator's registry clock and fill-recency check, PineAuto's "held since" stamp and MarketContext.refresh's default read Market.now instead of the system clock, so Zerodha's order times and the phone's "now" are never on two clocks; the paper-only period itself was right (it runs through its end date and is over the day after), proved by new PaperOnlyPeriodTest (ira-core) and PaperOnlyCalendarTest (app) for a Saturday end on the Sunday, a Sunday end on the Monday and a holiday end the next morning.
Tests given a fixed clock: 22 test classes (481 tests) had their wall-clock reads replaced, the 9 classes that use earlierToday and the 63 that use the fake Zerodha now get market-clock times through the helpers, and the process-wide pin covers the 82 field-level clock reads in 44 classes; all 279 app test classes start on the pinned clock.
Verified with the real Sunday clock: the five classes plus the new tests pass (716 tests of the *Live* set and every touched class, then 241 in the re-run of the affected classes), ira-core 3,928 and engine 1,534 tests pass with coverage verification; MAINTENANCE.md notes that tests never depend on the real calendar.
