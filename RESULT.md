# R130: the sweep → MSS → FVG video setup fails (research only; nothing in the app changed)

1. Boss's video setup (sweep a recent low, break the recent high, buy the pullback into the FVG on an engulfing candle) was tested as stated on all 5 indices, Aug 2021 to 19 Oct 2026. The signal was read on the index; the ATM option was bought.
2. It lost money in all 6 versions, after real charges and slippage, in the walk-forward AND in the locked holdout (read once).
3. As stated (V1, 5-min): 218 design trades, 45% won, -Rs 307 a trade, -Rs 66,966 on 1 lot. Holdout: 36 trades, 39% won, -Rs 750 a trade.
4. No version beat random entries with the same stop and target (best BH q 0.36). None came near Boss's 65% rule: the best win rate was 50%, on 14 trades.
5. The setup is rare: about 50 trades a year across 5 indices. The 1-minute version trades more and still loses (1,706 trades, -Rs 70 each).
6. "The target is hit more than the stop" is true on the index (124 vs 70), but only because the target sits about half the stop's distance away. Each trade loses 7.7 points on average.
7. "A sweep makes the break stronger" is false: it went the break's way first 50.7% of the time with a sweep (n 3,454) vs 52.9% without (n 6,808).
8. Only 46% of the FVG setups ever pulled back into the gap, and the engulfing filter made results worse (-Rs 307 vs -Rs 84 a trade).
9. Drawdown on 1 lot was -Rs 48k to -Rs 1.68 lakh by version, beyond the -Rs 40k account stop. No paper arm is proposed.
