# Four ideas borrowed from a day-trader kit (22 Oct 2026; CI fixes 25 Oct)

Boss, these four come from Jake Schincariol's day-trader skill (MIT, Oct 2026), rebuilt for IraAlgo. Branch `claude/borrow-day-trader`.

1. **Time machine on every bot ticket.** After each bot entry (paper and live, hidden bots too) the entry is replayed 2,000 times from the index's own past days already on the phone, at the same time of day, with the bot's stop and target. The ticket says how often the target or the stop came first, the break-even rate and a one-word verdict (edge / coin flip / against). It only reads what the phone holds on disk (the bundled and recorded sessions, else the history keeper's days), on its own low-priority thread; it never opens a network connection, and with nothing saved it says "no sessions saved yet" and does nothing else. It never skips, resizes or delays a trade. See it under each bot's row, in the Autopilot change timeline, in Copy diagnostics, or ask Jarvis "what did the time machine say". Its forward log is kept in the encrypted store and judged after 30 signal days (TODO R21).
2. **Order fingerprint.** Every check that clears an order stamps the exact order it judged. When the order is actually sent (paper and live), the sender recomputes the fingerprint and refuses if anything changed or the check is from another day, naming the field in a banner and the diagnostics. Closing a position is never refused.
3. **Three gates on a real order.** Your own real order shows: the checks passed and the fingerprint matches, a typed "yes" for that one order, your PIN. The typed yes is on by default (Settings → Zerodha → Real orders; turning it off asks your PIN, the PIN gate stays). A bot's live trade shows its own three on its row under Settings → Zerodha: Trade live with AI trades live, the PIN at arming, the fingerprint at the door. No existing gate is weakened.
4. **Flat-by.** Any bot position still open (MIS only) is closed at 15:25 for index options, 15:20 for stocks and funds, and 10 minutes before MCX's close, after the bot's own exit and the 15:05 expiry square-off had their chance. Positions held by design (overnight, NRML, the Expiry Put ticket) and your own trades are left alone; the kill switch never stops it. Times on Home → Automation card → Flat-by.

What none of them does: place a new order, change a size, read the network, or bypass the PIN, lock, Live, AI-live, guard, kill switch or day lock.

Fixed in this part (25 Oct, after CI on 22 Oct): two app test shards were red. The time machine's note on the Autopilot bus was waking the trade manager's next look, and that look reads the option's minute bars from the feed; in the tests the read ran on after the fake feed had closed and reached the real Upstox host, and it also ate the next test's scripted prices (the Liquidity entry-price test). Now the trade manager ignores the time machine's notes (words only, like the Candle agent's), the time machine reads the saved sessions in one streaming pass on its own thread, and a test proves no host is asked and no look is brought forward. Also: every fingerprint verdict is dated by the market clock the door reads back (the arms' own test clock on another day refused every entry at a weekend), and the test clock helper follows the market's trading day, so the suite is green on weekends and holidays too.

Code review of the whole branch (25 Oct) found and fixed: the flat-by (and the 15:05 expiry square-off) used to cancel a bot's resting stop and send the exit a minute later, while the bot put its stop back in between, so the two could loop until the close with the position never flat and no stop meanwhile; now the stop is cancelled, confirmed out, and the exit goes in the same pass, and a bot's own working exit or the other square-off's counts as cover and is never cancelled. MCX special sessions (the Diwali Muhurat hour, a Budget Sunday) get their flat-by ten minutes before their own close instead of 23:20. A price typed on the order review asks for "yes" again. The flat-by's look-up of a position's bot waits at most two seconds on the bots' lock. The time machine keeps only what the tickets can still show.
