# IraThinker review: 26 Oct 2026

Boss asked: "Check a new push for the IraThinker folder ... verify if it's working fine." This reviews `irathinker-app/` (v59,
commit 824f7ed) and how it talks to IraAlgo's ntfy alerts arm.

## In short

- **Secrets: clean.** There are no tokens, keystore, ntfy topic names, Dhan keys or personal data in the folder. The topics
  are generated on the phone at first run. the repository's token check (CLAUDE.md, "Repository hygiene") gives the same 2 lines as before
  (CLAUDE.md and one old note). the ntfy topic check finds nothing.
- **It never places orders.** The only hosts it talks to are Yahoo, Binance, NSE and BSE (option-chain probes), Google News
  and ntfy. No broker endpoint exists. The Dhan fields in Settings are stored and never used.
- **Harness: works, with one limit.** It compiles and runs, and gives 847 of 855 checks passing on fresh Yahoo data. The
  8 that fail are the "v48: ... unchanged" golden numbers, which only match Boss's original data file (not committed).
  After the fixes below: **870 pass, the same 8 golden checks fail, nothing else fails** (878 checks, 23 new). Same result
  in the India, UTC and New York time zones.
- **APK: builds.** The full pipeline built a signed 193 KB APK (target SDK 36), with two caveats:
  - The launcher icon PNGs are not in the repo, so the build used a stand-in icon.
  - The d8 in build-tools 34 crashes when run on JDK 21, so Google's R8 8.5.35 d8 was used instead.
  - Not installed on a phone or emulator.
- **Works:** the alert engine, the System Feed JSON, the Market Meter (display only), the mom60 and strangle paper tracks,
  price alerts and the ledger all behave as their tests say.
- **Doesn't work well:**
  - Background running on Android 15/16: see bugs 1–3. This is the biggest risk.
  - Expiries ignored holidays: fixed here.
  - Strategy alerts could fire on symbols with no NSE option: fixed here.
  - Stock strikes are guessed: this caused the INFY refusal.

### Not fixed: bigger changes, listed for Boss

| # | Severity | Bug | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **High** | **Android 15+ gives a `dataSync` foreground service 6 hours a day.** WatchService starts at 08:30 and has no `onTimeout()`. Around 14:30 Android stops it, and the app can crash. The watchdog stays dead until the app is opened, leaving only the slower job chain for the afternoon (mom60 exits, 15:20 reminder, end-of-day report). The APK targets SDK 36, so this applies. | Handle `onTimeout` with `stopSelf()`. Move the cadence to exact alarms plus a job chain, or to a service type without the cap. |
| B2 | **High** | **Boot start is forbidden on Android 15+** for `dataSync`. `BootReceiver` starts WatchService, which throws, and the watchdog is dead after a reboot until Boss opens the app. | From boot, schedule the job or alarm only. Start the service when the app is opened. |
| B3 | **High** | **No wake lock.** `WAKE_LOCK` is declared but never used. The ticks use `Handler.postDelayed`, whose clock stops while the CPU sleeps, so on an idle phone a pass can run minutes late (F2 now stops late alerts from going out stale). | `AlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle` plus a short partial wake lock per pass. |
| B4 | Medium | **Duplicate paper alerts.** Opening the Strategies screen runs the mom60 and strangle engines on a new thread, outside the engine's one-pass-at-a-time guard. Both can fire the same signal (a duplicate human alert and a duplicate feed message). | One lock around each engine's `check`. |
| B5 | Medium | **Stock strikes are guessed by price band** (10 below ₹2,000). Only DRREDDY, LT and BAJAJ-AUTO were checked against real chains. This is the INFY 1130 CE refusal; see Compatibility. | Use the NSE chain probe's listed strikes when it answers, or a strike-interval table. Never send a strike that isn't verified (send `strike: null` and let IraAlgo pick). |
| B6 | Medium | The holiday list covers **2026 only** (this push adds Republic Day, Tue 26 Jan 2027, which is January's last Tuesday, so the January expiry already shows Mon 25 Jan). From 1 Jan 2027 holidays are unknown, so the brief, reminder and end-of-day report go out on holidays and expiries won't shift. | Add 2027 when NSE publishes it, and show a warning when the year is missing. |
| B7 | Medium | **NSE and BSE cookies are never refreshed** once held, so after they expire the chain line drops out of alerts until a restart. The NSE endpoint may also be retired (NSE moved to `option-chain-v3`); not verified here. | Re-warm on every empty reply (at most every 10 minutes). Check the endpoint on the phone. |
| B8 | Medium | **Gold and commodity price alerts rarely fire** in the evening or at weekends: the NSE weekend and holiday gates and the 09:30–15:15 window block them. | Run the commodity price-alert pass outside the NSE gates. |
| B9 | Medium | **Stale gold is labelled live.** When Binance fails, the last chart is shown as "perpetual (live)" and can fire a price alert. | Add a stale flag, and skip alerts on it. |
| B10 | Medium | **Gold uses heavy data:** about 1 MB of 1-minute bars every 30–60 s during the scan, and every 5 minutes overnight. | Fetch only the new bars. |
| B11 | Medium | **Store files are rewritten in place** (journal, ledger, paper logs). A kill mid-write leaves bad JSON; `load()` returns empty, and the next save erases the history. | Write to a temp file and rename (`AtomicFile`). |
| B12 | Medium | **Privacy:** until GO PRIVATE is tapped, alerts also go to the guessable public topic, including rupee risk and the end-of-day paper P&L. `allowBackup="true"` sends the prefs (topics, Dhan token field) to cloud backup. | Leave rupee and P&L details out of public-topic posts, set `allowBackup="false"`, and don't collect the Dhan token until it's used. |
| B13 | Medium | **Feed messages can be forged.** Anyone who learns the feed topic can post a fake signal, because there is no auth or signature. | Use an ntfy access token or a self-hosted server, plus an HMAC field that IraAlgo checks. |
| B14 | Low | Thread pools are never shut down (Main/Detail). One file write runs on the main thread (Detail "paper" tap). ## Compatibility with IraAlgo (what IraThinker sends vs what `NtfyParser.signal` accepts)

| IraThinker message | What IraAlgo does | Right? | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| ORB_BREAKOUT / ORB_BREAKDOWN / GAP_FADE / MANUAL on a stock (`X.NS`) | Buys the named option if that exact strike and expiry are listed; `option.premium` caps the price at +5% | Mostly | See INFY below. The premium is a Black-Scholes guess at realised volatility, often below the real price, so expect "not chased" refusals. IraAlgo could show that reason plainly. |
| Same on ^NSEI / ^NSEBANK / ^CNXFIN | Accepted (FINNIFTY via `symbol: "FINNIFTY"`; IraAlgo's symbol list lacks `^CNXFIN`) | Yes | IraAlgo: add `^CNXFIN` → FINNIFTY. |
| **MOM60** (`paper: true`, BN / FINNIFTY / SENSEX) | **Bought**: IraAlgo ignores `paper`, `premiumStop` (30%) and `exitBy` (60-minute hold) | **No** | IraAlgo: honour `paper: true` (paper arm only, never the live path), or skip it. If kept, use `premiumStop` and `exitBy` as the stop and the exit time; otherwise its trade is not the mom60 rule. |
| **STRANGLE** (`side: "SELL_STRANGLE"`) | Refused: "a side not understood" | Refusing is right | IraAlgo never sells options. It should label this "information only (paper strangle, option selling is not traded)" instead of an error. IraThinker: none needed. |
| PRICE_ALERT | Refused: "a signal with no side" | Right | IraAlgo: label it information only. |
| MARKET_FLUSH | Refused: "not an index or NSE stock signal (no symbol)" | Right | IraAlgo: label it information only. |
| TEST (`test: true`) | Refused as a test | Right | None. |

### The three refusals from Boss's phone today

1. **"SELL_STRANGLE: a side not understood"**
   - This is correct behaviour. IraThinker mirrors every alert to the feed (Boss's all-topics rule), and the strangle is a
     paper track of option *selling*. IraAlgo only buys.
   - The fix is wording only, on the IraAlgo side: mark strategy STRANGLE / PRICE_ALERT / MARKET_FLUSH as information, not
     refusals.

2. **"INFY 1130 CE is not listed for 2026-10-27"**
   - The expiry was right: stock options are monthly, and the last Tuesday of October 2026 is the 27th, which is what
     IraThinker sent.
   - IraAlgo's code proves it found that expiry: it prints "not listed for <expiry>" only after the expiry is found, and
     refuses earlier with "the expiry asked for is not listed today".
   - **The strike was wrong.** IraThinker rounds a stock under ₹2,000 to a 10-point step (`OptionsMath.strikeStepForPrice`),
     but 1130 is not on INFY's NSE strike grid.
   - Fixes:
     - IraThinker (B5): send only verified strikes, or `strike: null`.
     - IraAlgo (describe only): for a stock signal whose exact strike is not listed, it could take the nearest listed strike
       on the same side of the money. That is a choice for Boss, because it changes the contract named. Or keep refusing,
       with the reason "strike not on the exchange grid".

3. **"stale: 8 minutes old (limit 8)" at 10:04, 10:27 and 11:42**
   - **IraAlgo read them late; IraThinker did not send late.**
   - Every feed message's `tsIST` is stamped by `SystemFeed.nowIst()` moments before the post (all 8 builders). The post runs
     on its own thread with 8-second timeouts. So a message is at most about 10–20 s older than its ntfy arrival time.
   - IraAlgo measures age from the earlier of `tsIST` and ntfy's time. An 8-minute age therefore means its reader picked the
     message up about 8 minutes after it arrived.
   - That fits the old reader: started at 09:15, slow reconnects, a once-a-minute decision pass. It was fixed in IraAlgo
     build 29ba08f (committed 11:58 IST, after all three times).
   - Two caveats:
     - If IraThinker runs on a different phone whose clock is behind, every message looks old. IraAlgo could compare `tsIST`
       with ntfy's own time and warn when they differ by more than 60 s.
     - IraThinker could *find* a signal late (B1–B3: no wake lock, the Android 15 6-hour cap). Before this push it would then
       send old levels with a fresh `tsIST`, which IraAlgo cannot detect. F2 now stops that. A future feed field `barIST`
       (the signal bar's time) would let IraAlgo check it too. It is not added here, to keep the wire format unchanged.
