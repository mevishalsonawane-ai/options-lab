Trading the study: three months with $100

Two rule sets built from the BTC and gold studies, tested walk-forward on the last three months. The rules and alarm thresholds were fixed from data before the window. Every trade pays costs, and nothing was tuned on the results shown here.

Neither account beat simply holding, and that was a hard test

BTC rose 41% in these three months (60,000 to 84,500), and gold rose about 6.5% after a 10% run in August. Both strategies are event traders, not trend followers. The BTC account held a directional position only 18% of the time, so it could not keep up with a steady rally.

Bitcoin: −$6.36, dragged down by the risk short

The four risk shorts all lost (−$4.16 in total): they fired on big-drop alarms during a bull market. The six breakout longs came to about −$0.93. Only 2 of them won, but the winner caught the 21 Sep surge (+2.8%). The weekly straddle sales lost $1.49 over seven weeks. Three of the seven weeks made money; the big loser was the 18–25 Sep week, when BTC jumped about 8% and the sale lost $4.33.

The alarm earned its keep where it mattered

The rules skipped the straddle sale on six Fridays because an alarm was high. One of those weeks, from 14 Aug, saw BTC rise 25.9%, and a straddle sold that Friday would have lost about $23. The other five skipped weeks would have made roughly $14 together, so the filter came out about $9 ahead. The alarm called big moves correctly but not always their direction: that 14 Aug week was flagged as a big drop and turned out to be a big rise.

Gold: +$1.64 over 3 months, +$4.48 in the last 30 days while gold fell 7%

The blow-off fade shorted gold seven times. It was wrong early in August's 10% rally (its worst trade was −3.9% on 19–21 Aug) and right when the rally broke: +3.7% from 27 Aug to 1 Sep, including +$3.15 on the 28 Aug fall. A buy-the-drop position carried in from June added +$1.46. No big down day happened in the window, so that rule had nothing new to buy. The account's worst fall was −7.8%, against −9.2% for simply holding gold.

What to change, and how to test it fairly

The evidence points to three changes:

  • Take the BTC risk short only when price is below its 200-day average: in the study, big-drop alarms in uptrends did not pay.
  • Keep the alarm filter on the straddle, since it avoided the only catastrophic week.
  • Give gold's buy-the-drop rule more history before judging it.

Any change made after seeing these three months has to be judged on the next three months, not this one. Otherwise the result is fitted to this period and worth little.
